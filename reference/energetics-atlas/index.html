Energetic systems as maps

Energetics Atlas

Six ways of reading a body, each drawn on its own terms. Put one at the center and lay the others onto it. A point lands where the center reads the same qualities it does, and anything the center cannot read waits below the map.

The modern mind looks for ultimate particles and thinks that it has isolated something more true and useful than a mere pattern.Wood, 2004, chapter 2
